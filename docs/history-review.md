# Settlement history review

The frozen review cohort is Hong Kong plus the 50 other records with the largest latest dated population in the pre-change catalog (QID lexicographic tie-break). The original population scopes are retained: some Wikidata records describe large municipalities or metropolitan regions rather than a single urban core. This is not a ranking of comparable urban populations.

**Scope:** 51 records examined against the linked government, museum or academic material. A review does not mean that every historical question was resolved. Unknown settlement dates and legal status dates remain unknown where the consulted material did not establish them. Dates below are conservative evidence bounds, not a claim to the earliest event that future research could find. Sources and the detailed rationale are retained in each card and the versioned override file.

All 21593 previous IDs and population year/value pairs are retained. Current catalog: 23231 records. Appearance bases: {'population-observation': 20446, 'unknown': 917, 'settlement': 1868}.

## Rules

- Earliest supported settlement or documented predecessor, with continuity explained. Regional archaeology alone is not a settlement date.
- Generic Wikidata P571 inception values are retained as unclassified events, never automatically treated as settlement foundation or legal city status. Conflicting first mentions are flagged, not minimized.
- A population observation may provide an earlier visibility bound; it never becomes a foundation. No population is invented before observations.
- Imprecise evidence appears at the upper bound. Wikidata century precision follows [Wikidata date semantics](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Help:Dates): for example, 1801–1900 is the nineteenth century. Raw BCE structured values remain unclassified pending calendar verification; reviewed BCE evidence uses explicit historical years without a year zero.
- The `founded` field remains a compatibility alias for `appearanceYear`; original date fields remain in `originalDate`. The separate override file is applied after every full, cached and legacy-only world import.
